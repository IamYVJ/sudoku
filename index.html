How to Play

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

  • Tap a cell, then a number to place it. Prefer the number first? Turn on Digit-first input in Settings.
  • Notes mode pencils in small candidates. Placing a digit clears it from the notes in the same row, column and box.
  • Fill pencils in every candidate for every empty cell at once (you can undo it).
  • Clashing digits turn red. In Strict mode (Settings), any wrong digit turns red, and three mistakes end the game.
  • Hint fixes a mistake first. Otherwise it fills the next logical step and tells you why, such as a hidden single in a box. For harder steps it points out the pair or pointing pattern and highlights its cells.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Easy to Hard never need guessing.
  • After a win, Challenge a friend copies a link to the same puzzle with your time to beat.
  • Works offline once loaded. To install it, use Install app in Settings, or on iPhone and iPad tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Diagonal is a variant you pick on the home screen: the two long diagonals (shaded on the board) must also hold 1 to 9 exactly once. Its stats and times are kept apart from Classic, and the Daily is always Classic.
  • The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone each day. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak, then share your result. Missed one? Past puzzles in the archive can still be solved and shared, but only on-the-day solves count toward the streak.
